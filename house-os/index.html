Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my lifetime purchase?
Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchase". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

Is my data synced between devices?
Yes — if iCloud sync is enabled in Settings, your inventory syncs across your devices via iCloud.

What's the difference between free and lifetime?
Free covers 10 items, 3 rooms, and 5 maintenance reminders. Lifetime ($29.99, one-time) removes all limits and all ads, forever.

Does lifetime cover my family?
Yes — the lifetime purchase supports Family Sharing.
