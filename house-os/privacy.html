Privacy Policy — House OS: Home Inventory

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

House OS is designed to keep your data yours. We do not require an account, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your home inventory — items, rooms, photos, receipts, warranties, and maintenance reminders — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud sync (below) or export it yourself (CSV/PDF export, which you initiate and control).

iCloud sync (optional)

If you enable iCloud sync in Settings, your inventory syncs across your devices via Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored on Apple's servers and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not operate our own servers and do not receive a copy of your synced data.

Advertising (free tier only)

The free version of House OS displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google's SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier and related diagnostic data to serve ads. Lifetime purchasers never see ads. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Lifetime purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment information. Purchase status is verified on-device via Apple's StoreKit.

Notifications

Maintenance reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. No reminder data is sent to any server.

Children

House OS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and note the change in the app's release notes.

Contact
