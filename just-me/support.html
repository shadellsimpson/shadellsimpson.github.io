FAQ

How does the check work?

Tap "Run check" and the app tests your internet connection, DNS, and download speed on your device, then compares that with live outage reports for the service you're asking about. You'll get a straight answer: it's you, it's them, or everything's clear.

Do I need an account?

No. Just Me? has no accounts and no sign-in. Your follows, settings, and activity stay on your device.

What happens when I report an issue?

Your report is sent anonymously (service, issue type, time, and an optional ZIP code) and counted toward the live Area Pulse totals everyone sees. Reports are never displayed publicly with any personal information.

Why am I seeing ads?

Just Me? is free, and ads keep it that way. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Clear activity in the app, or simply delete the app. Server-side report counts contain no personal data.
